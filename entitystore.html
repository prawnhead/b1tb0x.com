EntityStore v3 · the doublets data model

Everything is a GUID in one of three tables. The table an id lives in says what it is: a word is its text; an entity is just an id, described by the doublets that point at it; a doublet is a pair. A fact is two doublets.

schema

columns

words.id
GUID, primary key.
words.value
the text; UNIQUE, never empty. Case-sensitive, found or created.
entities.id
GUID, primary key — the whole row. Meaning comes from its doublets.
doublets.id
GUID, primary key.
doublets.left
a GUID referencing any row in words, entities or doublets.
doublets.right
likewise. UNIQUE(left, right) so a pair exists once; indexed on right.

built on top

A fact is two doublets: ((subject, predicate), object). The inner (subject, predicate) is shared by every object.
Text is a chain of positions: each position an entity, with (pos, of) → word and (pos, next) → pos.
Built-in entities (fixed GUIDs, named like anything else): a · name · of · next · word separator.
